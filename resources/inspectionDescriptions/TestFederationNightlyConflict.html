Reports JUnit Jupiter test classes and functions that are nightly tests (@Tag("nightly"), e.g. @NightlyTest) and also smoke (@Tag("smoke")) or contract (@Tag("contract:<Domain>")) tests.

Nightly tests are skipped in remote and safe-merge runs, so the smoke or contract schedule never applies before merging.

Suppress with @Suppress("TestFederationNightlyConflict") on the function, class or file.
